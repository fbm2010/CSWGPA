✦ How Weighted GPA Works

A weighted GPA gives extra credit for challenging courses. Honors classes typically add +0.5 points, while AP, IB, and Dual Enrollment courses add +1.0 point to the base grade value before averaging.

An unweighted GPA uses the standard 4.0 scale for all courses regardless of difficulty. Both are important — colleges look at both when evaluating transcripts.

Your GPA is calculated as the sum of (grade points × credit hours) divided by total credit hours.
